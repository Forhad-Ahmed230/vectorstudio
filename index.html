Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about GravityLab? Find your answers here.

What is GravityLab?

+
GravityLab is a comprehensive suite built specifically for creators, offering high-speed AI prompt configurations, automatic layout slicing, vectorizing nodes, and custom Chrome Extension sync bridges.

How does the Google Flow integration work?

+
When you run the GravityLab Chrome Extension alongside the workspace player, it directly passes generation prompts and listens to background updates, eliminating manual copy-pasting loops.

Can I use the generated icons commercially?

+
Yes! All vectors and asset designs generated using GravityLab are fully yours to use across commercial client projects, templates, apps, or print layouts.

How do I upgrade to the Pro plan?

+
Simply sign in, go to the profile settings, and click Upgrade. Upgrades are processed instantly via our secure merchant partner with instant billing activation.
